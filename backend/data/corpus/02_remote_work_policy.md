# Remote Work Policy — Northfield Analytics (fictional)

This is a fictional internal policy document used as sample content for
this corpus.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible to apply
for a remote work arrangement. Approval is granted by the employee's
direct manager and is reviewed every 6 months.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home-office
stipend to approved remote employees. Additional equipment requests are
evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

## Working Hours

Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours, 10:00
AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone, and to attend all scheduled team
meetings with video on unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

## Paid Leave

Employees are entitled to twenty-four (24) paid leave days during each
completed calendar year, in addition to twelve (12) separate sick leave
days. Paid leave and sick leave do not carry over to the next calendar
year.
