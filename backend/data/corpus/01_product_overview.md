# Veridoc Cloud Backup — Product Overview

Veridoc Cloud Backup is a fictional SaaS product used as sample content
for this corpus. All company names, prices, and figures below are made up
for demonstration purposes.

## Plans and Pricing

| Plan | Monthly Price | Storage | Devices |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $5 | 100 GB | 1 |
| Plus | $12 | 1 TB | 3 |
| Pro | $25 | 5 TB | 10 |

The Basic plan does not include automatic versioning. The Plus and Pro
plans keep the last 30 days of file versions automatically.

## Free Trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No
credit card is required to start the trial. If no payment method is
added before the trial ends, the account is automatically downgraded to
a read-only state until a plan is selected.

## Cancellation

A subscription can be cancelled at any time from Account Settings. Data
is retained for 30 days after cancellation before permanent deletion,
except on the Basic plan, where data is retained for 7 days.
